Our paper <a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=WLNBGbEQnV">Measuring Progress in Diffusion Language Model Pretraining</a> has been accepted at the NeurIPS 2026 <a href="https://bento-neurips.github.io/">BeNTo workshop</a>! We propose a protocol to check which metrics reliably track progress during diffusion language model pretraining, and release an open-source <a href="https://github.com/agonon/speedrun-dlm">speedrun</a> built around downstream HellaSwag accuracy.
